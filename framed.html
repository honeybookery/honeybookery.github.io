A middle-grade mystery

Framed

What happens when the principal is not your pal?

  • Ages 8–12
  • Diary-style novel
  • Mystery

She didn't cheat. But that doesn't matter when everyone, including the principal, has already decided she did.

Told through diary entries, Framed follows a student who is wrongly accused, and who starts digging for the truth. What she uncovers about the person in charge is bigger than she ever expected.
